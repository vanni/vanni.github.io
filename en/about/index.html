About

1 min · Vanni | Translations:
  • It

Hi, I’m Vanni, the owner and author of iBloggy.net.

This is a little corner of the internet where I share my life in Australia as an expat.

My wife and I thought it would be nice to use this space to collect and share all the moments that make our journey exciting, sometimes challenging, but always meaningful.

From renovating our home to the ups and downs of everyday life, iBloggy is where we gather the stories that shape our experience in Australia and connect with anyone who would like to follow us on this journey.

Technology used#

This blog was built with Hugo, one of the most popular open-source static site generators. I could have used WordPress or another CMS with all sorts of plugins and bells and whistles, but as they say, “less is more”. The beauty of a static site like this, built with Hugo, is its speed, simplicity and, above all, security, as there is no database to manage.
