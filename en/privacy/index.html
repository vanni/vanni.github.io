Privacy

2 min · Vanni | Translations:
  • It

This personal blog is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process and log your IP address for security purposes. For more information, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views and page performance. The beacon sends navigation and timing metrics to Cloudflare. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data and does not track end users across its customers’ websites. For details, see its information on data collection and Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Comments on blog posts are provided by EchoThread. Loading the widget connects your browser to EchoThread’s CDN. If you submit a comment, EchoThread receives the comment and technical data, including your IP address and user-agent, for moderation and abuse prevention. It may also receive profile details you provide or use to sign in. Published comments are public. See EchoThread’s Privacy Policy.

This site does not use advertising services. Every page also loads the Buy Me a Coffee button script from its CDN; this connection may involve processing technical data such as your IP address. If you interact with the button, Buy Me a Coffee’s privacy practices apply.

If you send a message through the contact form, the details you enter are sent to Web3Forms so they can forward them to me by email. Web3Forms says it processes data on servers in the United States. See Web3Forms’ privacy information.

Search runs in your browser; your search terms are not sent to me. External links lead to websites with their own privacy policies.

This notice may be updated if the site’s features change.
