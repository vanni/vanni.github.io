Where It all began

November 20, 2026 · 1 min · Vanni | Translations:
  • It

As Aldo would say: “I can’t believe it!” And yes, on 12 January 2027, I’ll celebrate 20 years since arriving here in Australia.

I still remember it as if it were yesterday—well, if I’m honest, not quite—but I’ll try to tell you about it.

I arrived in Brisbane with plenty of ideas, dreams to pursue and a new life to plan.

It certainly wasn’t easy. There were many sacrifices to make and decisions to take, without a deep understanding of Australian institutions or familiar faces I could turn to for advice. But I had a solid plan: I was on the other side of the world to reinvent myself, and my first goal was to try to obtain citizenship. Eligibility was based on a points system calculated from factors such as education, age and work experience.

I admit I had moments of discouragement, but in the end I never gave up. With the stubbornness of a true Bergamasco, I got there. I still had to graduate, complete another year of study through the ACS (Australian Computer Society) and do an internship. After that, I had enough points to apply for citizenship.

I’ll leave it there for now, but I’ll come back to this topic later.
