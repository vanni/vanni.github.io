TV Series: Science Fiction

November 25, 2026 · 2 min · Vanni | Translations:
  • It

How about you? Unless a movie is especially gripping, I often find it too long to watch in one evening. I don’t always make it to the end and end up falling asleep on the couch. Thankfully, there are wonderful TV series, which I love and find much easier to watch after a long day.

I especially enjoy science fiction, spy dramas, and crime series (British ones are best). Every now and then, I also watch something lighter, just for a change.

This week: science fiction#

This week, I finished the third season of Silo. After a somewhat heavy-going second season, I thought this latest one was incredible: it clarified many details and events from earlier seasons, which finally make sense. For me, it was a fantastic season! Now we wait for the fourth, due to arrive on July 9, 2027, on Apple’s streaming platform. Watch the trailer.

More recommendations#

  • Battlestar Galactica — A little dated, but a must-watch for science-fiction fans. Watch the trailer.
  • For All Mankind — A great series now in its fifth season, with one final season expected next year to wrap up the story. It presents an alternate reality where the space race and rivalry between superpowers take a different course. It’s also available on Apple’s streaming platform. Watch the trailer.
  • Foundation — A great series now in its third season. The storyline is quite complex, so I’ll let you discover it for yourself. Available on Apple’s streaming platform. Watch the trailer.

Honorable mention#

  • Severance — A very unusual series, sometimes a little slow but fascinating in its concept. A procedure separates a person’s work memories from their personal ones, creating two distinct identities for each employee: the “innie,” who knows nothing about the outside world, and the “outie,” who lives outside work without knowing anything about their job. Available on Apple’s streaming platform. Watch the trailer.
  • Upload — Set in 2033, Upload imagines a future where people can “upload” their consciousness to a digital afterlife when their bodies die. The experience can be more or less ideal, depending on how much they pay. Available on Hulu. Watch the trailer.

Have a TV series to recommend? Send me a message via the Contact page; I’d love to hear your suggestions.
