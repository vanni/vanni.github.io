Vegemite

What is it?#

According to Wikipedia, Vegemite is a salty spread made from yeast extract. It’s usually spread on bread, but can also be used in cooking. In other words, it’s a kind of jam without the sweetness—and with no intention of making you happy on the first taste. In Australia, it’s practically a national institution: if you’ve never tried it, people might look at you as if you’d just landed from another planet.

My first taste#

I tried it for the first time soon after arriving here, way back in 2007. The result? I didn’t like it. Not one bit. I still remember the look on my face after that first bite: the look of someone who’d just discovered that bread could betray them. As a good Italian, used to Nutella, jam, and all the other things that make breakfast a happy moment, I just couldn’t understand how Australians could eat that stuff with such satisfaction.

The revelation…#

Then, as often happens, the wisdom of the locals came to the rescue—this time in the form of my wife, who revealed Vegemite’s great secret: you have to spread it in a very thin layer. I mean really thin! This isn’t Nutella, where more is always better. Vegemite has such an intense flavour that a little too much can turn an ordinary breakfast into an experience that makes you rethink all your life choices. After years of living with this mysterious spread, I realised the trick isn’t so much learning to love it as learning to respect it—and, above all, not spreading it like jam!

Would you try Vegemite, or have you already tried it? I’d love to read your comments.
