Why Buy Me a Coffee?

November 23, 2026 · 1 min · Vanni | Translations:
  • It

This time, I’m taking things seriously: a new domain, a new platform and a personal investment that I intend to keep up over the long term.

I’ve looked into how I could support the site and make it self-sustaining, so I can focus on creating content.

I don’t really like advertising through banners (Google AdSense), either for its appearance or as a concept, so I haven’t considered it for now. Instead, I’ve decided to try Buy Me a Coffee, a platform that lets readers support creators with a small, voluntary contribution. The amount is entirely up to you, and there’s no obligation. It would be lovely to eventually cover the site’s costs through this kind of support and perhaps offer other types of content on other platforms.

That’s all for now. Thanks in advance to anyone who chooses to make a small contribution to keep the blog online.
