An invitation to you all

Welcome once again to my personal blog. This space on the web will share a little about my life here in Australia. I’m building it with Hugo, one of the most popular open-source static site generators. For the theme, I chose PaperMod. I like its clean style and lack of unnecessary frills. From ChatGPT: Static sites, such as those built with Hugo, offer many advantages over traditional CMS platforms like WordPress. Because they’re generated directly as HTML, CSS and JavaScript, they deliver high performance, very fast load times and a smoother browsing experience. They don’t require a database or server-side processing, which also makes them more secure, less exposed to attacks and easier to maintain. They’re also easy to version and manage with Git, and can be published automatically using tools such as GitHub Actions. The result is a modern, lightweight, scalable and affordable solution—ideal for anyone who wants a fast, reliable website that’s easy to maintain.

Where It all began

As Aldo would say: “I can’t believe it!” And yes, on 12 January 2027, I’ll celebrate 20 years since arriving here in Australia. I still remember it as if it were yesterday—well, if I’m honest, not quite—but I’ll try to tell you about it. I arrived in Brisbane with plenty of ideas, dreams to pursue and a new life to plan. It certainly wasn’t easy. There were many sacrifices to make and decisions to take, without a deep understanding of Australian institutions or familiar faces I could turn to for advice. But I had a solid plan: I was on the other side of the world to reinvent myself, and my first goal was to try to obtain citizenship.
